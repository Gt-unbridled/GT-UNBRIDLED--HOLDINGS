Brand guidelines

Everything needed to present Gt-unbridled Holdings consistently: logo, colour, type, voice and usage.

Logo

The open ring is the mark. It stays open on purpose: unbridled, and always room to grow. Use the gold ring on dark backgrounds and the deeper gold on light ones.

Logo for darkLogo for lightMark only

Colour

Select a swatch to copy its code.

Typography

Fraunces

Headlines and brand moments. Weights 600 and 800, tight leading.

System sans

Body text and interface. Clean, fast and readable on every device, at 17px with generous line height.

Voice

Plain, confident and specific. We say what we do and what happens next.

Do

Write in sentence case and active voice. Name the outcome. Keep sentences short.

Do not

Use hype, jargon or em dashes. Promise results we cannot show. Shout in capitals.

Using the logo

  • Keep clear space around it equal to the mark's width.
  • Do not go below 24px tall for the mark.
  • Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects.
  • Place it on plain backgrounds with enough contrast.
  • Write the name as Gt-unbridled Holdings, then Gt-unbridled.
